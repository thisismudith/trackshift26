09GROUNDED, NOT HYPED

What we deliberately don't claim.

✕

No battery telepathy

We never claim to reconstruct a car's proprietary ERS state of charge. The twin is explicitly labeled SIMULATED.

✕

No over-claimed validation

The real Hamilton–Antonelli position flip anchors the realism of the scenario — not the simulated energy state. We say so on screen.

✕

No novelty over-claim

Fieni et al. already model interacting F1 agents and game-theoretic energy. Our contribution is the missing operational layer: causal advice under hidden rival state, 2026 rule discontinuities, and visible provenance in one loop.

✕

No live equilibrium solving

No Nash or Stackelberg computation per telemetry update — a bounded, rule-masked beam search that targets pit-wall latency.

✕

No DRS-field relabeling

The public telemetry drs channel is never presented as 2026 Active Aero state. The disclaimer stays visible in the demo.

✕

No LLM in the decision loop

Explanations come from deterministic templates over top scoring factors. Every recommendation is reproducible and testable.
